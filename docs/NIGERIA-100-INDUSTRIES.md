# Nigeria: 100 private-sector opportunity categories

Updated 8 October 2026. The accompanying [CSV](NIGERIA-100-INDUSTRIES.csv) contains exactly **100 distinct private-sector business, professional, and career categories**, with **65 relevant website URLs across 62 normalized hostnames**. Government bodies, regulator registries, public-institution lanes, and government websites were removed as prospect or platform targets. The original source-based review and passive-owner workflow architecture remains intact.

This is a practical opportunity map, not an official or exhaustive industry classification, a list of 100 separate platforms, or a claim of 100 connected integrations. Related categories can share a suitable private marketplace, company, association, or business service.

## The audience

The map centres on private business owners and legally operated individual businesses: farmers and commodity traders; manufacturers; property agents and developers; independent professionals; private schools and healthcare businesses; creative studios; hotels and travel operators; recruiters and career service providers; technology, energy and logistics companies; car dealers and workshops; and independent retail sellers. A privately operated business can serve regulated or public-sector-adjacent work without making a government body a GTM target.

Jiji is one example for retail and classified sellers. It is not the architecture for every industry. Real-estate owners may prefer PropertyPro or Nigeria Property Centre; agricultural businesses may use AFEX or Hello Tractor; career workflows may begin with Jobberman or MyJobMag. These are researched candidates and manual handoffs, not connected services.

## Read the rows correctly

- **Website URL:** a relevant real private source or service. It may be a direct company website rather than a directory of prospects. Its existence does not grant API access or reuse permission.
- **Useful signal or workflow:** a proposed owner benefit, not a feature claimed to be live. Some sources assist with sourcing, partner research, service enquiries, or careers rather than buyer discovery.
- **Access status:** observed access evidence and remaining requirements. No row is marked connected. Public pages, advertised integrations, and documented APIs are different levels of evidence.
- **Source URL:** the primary official page supporting relevance or access. Research used live retrieved pages or current official search-index content. Retrieval limitations are disclosed where relevant. No customer account, private dataset, or authenticated API call was tested.

Inventory listings are supply signals. An open vacancy is a hiring signal. A company project announcement is company context. None automatically proves a purchase need for the owner's offer. Audience fit, actual need, contact permission, source freshness and outcomes must remain separate.

## The passive experience

Ask once what the owner offers, the locations served, and whether they want customers, suppliers, partners or career opportunities. Start with their actual enquiries and authorized business records. Show one clear next action; expose setup details only when useful.

After a permitted source connection exists, deliver a quiet digest of meaningful new items. Show the source, observed date, last successful update, why the item fits, missing evidence, and a concrete next action. Respect state/city and service-radius preferences across Nigeria. Offer adjustable frequency, pause, duplicate suppression and a cost budget.

For sources without approved automated access, provide a clear manual handoff. Reminding an owner about their own recorded task can be automatic inside Pull without claiming the external site is synchronized. Retain stale records visibly when a refresh fails; never manufacture fresh results.

Success means higher owner-judged relevance, less time to a useful action, fewer stale or unsupported claims, controlled cost, genuine replies and verified outcomes. A simpler UI or a longer source list alone does not prove market-leading performance. See [global private-sector architecture](GLOBAL-PRIVATE-SECTOR.md) for the same model across regions.
